Signs You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Tyler

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.

Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
